About me

A compassionate, evidence-informed approach

Psychotherapy that respects both the individual and the cultural context in which their story unfolds.

I have worked in counselling since 2017, beginning at the Near East Counselling Centre. Since 2020, I have provided psychotherapy to both face-to-face and online clients.

My approach is integrative. I orient my practice toward Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and Positive Transcultural Psychotherapy, while drawing on evidence-based psychological interventions according to each client's needs.

I have experience working with clients experiencing PTSD, generalized anxiety, depression, schizophrenia, borderline personality disorder, bipolar disorder and other psychological difficulties. I also work with people who may not have a specific diagnosis but are struggling with relationships, life circumstances, emotional wellbeing or personal concerns.

Having worked with people from African, European and Middle Eastern cultural backgrounds, I value cultural understanding as an important part of effective therapeutic work.
