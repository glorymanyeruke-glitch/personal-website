About me

I have worked in counselling since 2017, beginning at the Near East Counselling Centre. Since 2020, I have provided psychotherapy to clients face to face and online.

My approach is integrative. I work mainly with Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and Positive Transcultural Psychotherapy, and draw on other evidence-based interventions according to what each person needs.

I have worked with people from African, European and Middle Eastern cultural backgrounds, and I see cultural understanding as an important part of effective therapy.

How I work

Structured psychological methods, with attention to the whole person and their cultural context.

Integrative

Different therapeutic perspectives and interventions can be combined to respond to each person’s needs and goals.

Evidence-informed

I value approaches supported by research, and keep developing my knowledge through professional education and specialist training.

Culturally responsive

Culture, identity, relationships and life context matter. I aim to offer a space that is respectful, collaborative and culturally aware.

What I can help with

  • Anxiety and worry
  • Low mood and depression
  • Trauma and PTSD
  • Bipolar disorder
  • Schizophrenia
  • Borderline personality disorder
  • Relationships and life transitions

You don’t need a diagnosis to start. I also work with people who are struggling with relationships, life circumstances, emotional wellbeing or personal concerns.
